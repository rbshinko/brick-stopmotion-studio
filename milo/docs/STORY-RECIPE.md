# Story recipe (from a study of 10 top animated story Shorts)

Formats: A "Why are we scared of X?" power-fantasy rant (hook Q 0–2 s; fear-flipping rule 2–9 s; 4–5 escalating attacks ~3.5–4 s each: 2nd-person setup + 1–2 short 1st-person comebacks; flip at ~75–80% where the threat wants out, "Nah, you're not leaving until…"; role-swap epilogue last 3 s, loop to shot 1). A2 "Why are X like this" situation rant (3–5 chronological stages). B numbered list (3 items ~9 s, ends with question to viewer → 4–8x comments/view). C first-person rise story (sincere, callback).

Hook: 9/10 open with a question; 8/10 start "Bro,"; speech at 0.0 s; first sentence 7–14 words (median 8), ends ~2.2 s; = title read aloud. First punchline ~6 s.
Pacing: 117–173 words, ~230 wpm, speech 79–90% of runtime; 13–19 sentences, mean 9.3 words (median 8); long setup → short comeback rhythm; median inter-sentence gap 0.24 s, only 2% > 0.5 s. ~1.2 cuts per sentence; shots median ~1.5 s, 14–26/video; 55% of cuts within 0.3 s of a sentence start (2x chance). Jokes ~2–2.6 per 10 s.
Voice: "you" are the hero + "I" comebacks; confident unbothered kid; slang Bro/Nah/little bro/cooked/lock in; comebacks = ordinary kid life vs threat (chores, rent, wifi, mom).
Endings: role reversal (5), pure punchline (2), question to viewer (lists), emotional callback (1). Voice ends 0.1–0.3 s before video end; no outro silence.
Titles: "Why are we scared of X?" median 6.3M; lists best engagement; "Why are X like this" 4.6M; 3rd-person "He/She… 😳" stories weakest 0.5M. CAPS word in title 6.3M vs 2.0M; "??" 5.7M vs 3.0M. ≤28 s did worse.

RECIPE for Milo: 30–34 s, 120–130 words, 14–17 sentences; alternate setup (10–14 w) / comeback (3–7 w); hook ≤2.2 s ≤9 words = title; first joke by 5–6 s; 4 escalations ~4 s; flip 22–26 s; ending role swap that loops to shot 1, or every 3rd video ask the viewer; cut on every sentence start, shots 1.2–1.8 s, comebacks ~1 s; titles "Why is everyone in this town SCARED of X??" style, one CAPS word, "??".
